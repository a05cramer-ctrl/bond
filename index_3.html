Rules

Where the money goes

Bonds only

The wallet pays bail. It does not pay people, lawyers, fines or anything else.

Paid to the court

Payments go to the clerk of court or a licensed bondsman, never as cash to a person.

Public wallet

Anyone can check the balance and every transaction on Solana.

Receipt for every bond

Each payment is posted with its date, amount, county and transaction.

No names, no photos

Nobody's arrest is ever posted. Help shouldn't follow someone around.

Small bonds first

The wallet goes furthest on the low bonds that keep people inside for weeks.

Questions

FAQ

Who gets helped?

People held on low bonds. Payments go straight to the court or a licensed bondsman.

Why no names?

An arrest is not a conviction, and posting it on a coin site would follow people long after they're out. Receipts show the date, amount, county and transaction only.

Can I check the money?

Yes. The bail wallet is public on Solana, and the balance on this page is read from it live.

What happens to bail money after the case?

When a court returns a bond at the end of a case, it goes back into the wallet and pays the next one.
